# MINDVERSE Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 23, 2026  
**Effective date:** November 23, 2026  

## Introduction

MINDVERSE ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application MINDVERSE ("the App").

The App is developed and published by Mir Md. Masum. You can contact us at **mirmasum@mail.com**.

## Information We Do Not Collect

MINDVERSE is an **offline-first puzzle game**. We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data from your device. Specifically:

- We do **not** collect personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, etc.).
- We do **not** collect device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID, etc.).
- We do **not** collect location data.
- We do **not** collect photos, media, or files from your device.
- We do **not** collect contacts or calendar data.
- We do **not** collect app activity or usage analytics.
- We do **not** collect diagnostic data or crash reports.
- We do **not** share any data with third parties.

## Data Stored Locally

The App stores the following information **only on your device**:

- **Game progress:** Your completed levels, scores, stars, and unlocks.
- **In-app purchases:** Records of purchases made through Google Play to restore your entitlements.
- **Settings:** Your preferred language, theme, and accessibility settings.
- **Daily challenges:** Your daily challenge completion history and streak data.

This data is stored locally on your device and is **not transmitted** to any server or third party. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or using the "Reset All Progress" feature within the App.

## In-App Purchases

MINDVERSE offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play. When you make a purchase:

- Google processes the payment and provides a receipt to the App.
- The App stores a local record of your purchase to restore your entitlements.
- No payment information is collected or stored by us.
- Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) governs how Google handles your payment data.

## Children's Privacy

MINDVERSE is rated **Everyone** and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently received personal data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App and updating the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data, including:

- The right to access, correct, or delete your data.
- The right to restrict or object to processing.
- The right to data portability.

Since MINDVERSE does not collect personal data, these rights are largely not applicable. However, you can delete all locally stored game data at any time using the "Reset All Progress" feature in the App settings.
